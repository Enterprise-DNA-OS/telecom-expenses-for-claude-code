# Why there is no front end

This desk turns carrier invoices, service references, allocations and disputes into questions an operator can answer with a coding agent. Read-only HTML gives finance a review sheet and procurement a renewal brief. No server is needed for those files.

Calero also supplies integrations, carrier services and operational workflows. The free base covers internal expense review using imported and operator-maintained records. It does not collect bills from carriers, provide mobile workflows, execute orders, pay invoices or manage devices. Screens can provide point-and-click review, live collaboration, mobile and offline workflows that this local desk does not provide.

Keep those services in place until replacements have been scoped and tested. Enterprise DNA can build the required screens and connections around your version. The base works with Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode or Cursor. Agent subscriptions and hosting are separate.

Your editable rules are the reason to own the database: a notice period, allocation rule or review query can change with your business. The ten supplied questions run today. Calero has reporting of its own; these are not claims that it cannot answer equivalent questions.
